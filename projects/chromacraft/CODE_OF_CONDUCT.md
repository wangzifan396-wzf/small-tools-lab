# Code of Conduct

ChromaCraft is committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone.

Be considerate, communicate constructively, respect differing experiences, and focus criticism on ideas and behavior. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior undermines the community. Reports should be sent privately to the maintainers through the repository's security contact. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
